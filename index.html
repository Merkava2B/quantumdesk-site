Operating principles

AI should earn the right to act.

Capability is not authority. Good systems separate evidence, judgment, permission and execution.

Evidence first Trace conclusions back to sources, provenance and observable facts.
Uncertainty visible Make ambiguity explicit instead of hiding it behind confident language.
Authority scoped Define who or what can act, under which conditions, and with what limits.
Human control Preserve meaningful human approval and intervention where consequences matter.
Evidence→ Provenance→ Judgment→ Risk & uncertainty→ Authority→ Controlled execution→ Audit trail
